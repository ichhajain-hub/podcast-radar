How a Monday run works

  1. Trigger. Vercel Cron calls /api/run?mode=weekly at 5:30 AM IST (daily runs refresh the dashboard the same way, without email).
  2. Read config. The orchestrator loads the active shows and weights Aditya set in Settings. No code or redeploy needed.
  3. Fetch. One job per show pulls live RSS or YouTube-channel feeds: the last 30 days for India, 14 days for global. Episodes already stored are skipped.
  4. Extract. Gemini Flash reads titles and show notes and returns guests, topics with stance, and brands, each with a short evidence quote.
  5. Deep-read. For India episodes with a public YouTube video, Gemini Flash watches it at low resolution and lists spoken sponsor reads with timestamps.
  6. Resolve. Gemini Flash merges name variants of people, themes and brands. Duplicates are linked, never deleted.
  7. Score. score(from, to, weights) applies the gates and weights and tags each item New, Rising or Repeat against last week.
  8. Find links. Gemini Flash with Google Search grounding finds LinkedIn and company sites for shortlisted guests, and keeps only URLs from search results.
  9. Write and deliver. Gemini Flash writes the "why now" lines from each item's own sources (fact-checked in code). The brief is stored, emailed through Resend and shown on the dashboard.
